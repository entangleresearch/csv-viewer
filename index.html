A SIMPLE WORKFLOW

How to use CSV by eRL

Everything you need to search literature, inspect records and build a clean dataset — with no login or software installation.

01

Search the dataset

Enter words such as quantum, machine learning or an author's name. Put double quotes around a phrase to search it exactly.

02

Focus on a field

Choose research content, every field, visible fields or one specific column — for example Document Title or Author Keywords.

03

Combine conditions

Expand Advanced filters. Combine rules with AND or OR, such as publication year ≥ 2024 and citations greater than 5.

04

Choose visible columns

Use Essential, Research or All fields presets, or tick columns individually. The results table immediately updates.

05

Sort and inspect

Click a table header to sort. Click any paper row to inspect its abstract, authors, affiliation, DOI and other metadata.

06

Export or import

Export only matching rows and selected columns. Import another comma-separated CSV using the button or drag-and-drop.

Try a sample search

Open the explorer with the keyword “quantum” already entered. You can refine it with a publication year filter.

Useful search tips

Separate words to require all terms. Use quotation marks for exact phrases. Searches are case-insensitive.

Combine keyword search and advanced conditions. To search only displayed columns, choose “Visible columns” in the search scope.

Tip: press Ctrl / Cmd + K to focus the search box.

Your data and privacy

The built-in dataset is packaged with the page; the explorer works offline. Imported files are processed in your own browser.

Search and selection are local. External links to DOI and PDF providers require internet access.

If you publish this website publicly, its embedded paper metadata is accessible to every visitor.
